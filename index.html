What is this app?

Transformation Tracker is a 100-day photo challenge app. You take one progress photo per day, and over time you build a visual record of your body transformation — all stored privately on your device.

How it works
1
Set your challenge
Choose how many days — 30, 60, 100, 180, or 365. You can change this anytime in Settings.
2
Take a photo daily
Tap the + button each day. Same pose, same lighting. The ghost overlay helps you match your previous shot.
3
Watch yourself change
Check your Progress tab to see every photo. Use the Comparison screen to see how far you've come.
Privacy policy

All your photos and data are stored only on this device. Nothing is ever uploaded to any server. We don't collect any personal information, analytics, or usage data. When you delete the app or clear your browser data, everything is gone — no backups exist on our end.

Feedback & support

Built with ❤️ to help you stay consistent and see real results. If you have feedback, run into a bug, or just want to share your progress — reach out anytime.
